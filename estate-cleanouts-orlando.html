What we hold

Your dad's garage isn't a chore. It's forty years of Saturday mornings.

Heirlooms are set aside. Anything of value is documented. Everything else is rehomed with respect — donated or passed on, never treated as nothing.

How it works

The process

Three quiet steps.

Unhurried, and entirely on your terms. You can be as involved — or as removed — as you need to be.

i.

The Walkthrough

We listen first. You tell us what matters, what's tender, and what you'd rather not handle yourself. We make a plan around that — no clipboards, no pressure.

ii.

The Sort

Four gentle piles: keep, family, donate, and let go. Nothing leaves without your say-so.

And an "unsure" box that's always honored — anything you're not ready to decide on waits for you.

iii.

The Reveal

A clear, calm room handed back to you. Space to breathe, and one less weight to carry through everything else.
