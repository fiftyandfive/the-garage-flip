Most Orlando garages fall between $600 and $1,800.

The spread comes down to two things: how big the garage is, and whether you want it simply cleared or cleared and rebuilt.

Garage cleanout pricing in Orlando is quoted one of two ways — by the hour, or as a fixed price for the whole job. Hourly sounds cheaper until the day runs long. A fixed quote, agreed in writing before anyone starts, means the number you hear is the number you pay.

What moves the price

Four things change the number.

i.

Volume, not just floor space

A two-car garage stacked to the rafters takes longer than a three-car with a clear middle. What is in the room matters as much as the room.

ii.

Disposal and donation runs

Hauling, dump fees, and donation drop-offs are built into the quote. Heavy debris — old tile, paint, construction leftovers — costs more to dispose of than household clutter.

iii.

Sorting versus hauling

If everything goes, the job is fast. If most of it needs a keep-or-let-go decision, that is real labor — and it is the part most worth paying for.

iv.

Organization after the clear-out

Racks, wall systems, and labeled bins are materials plus installation. This is the entire gap between the Reset and the Refresh.

Before you book anyone

Three questions worth asking.

Is the quote fixed, or an estimate? An estimate can move on the day. A fixed quote in writing cannot. Ask which you are being given.

Does it include disposal and donation? Some cleanout companies quote labor and bill dump fees separately. Confirm the number is all-in.

What happens if the scope genuinely changes? The honest answer is that the crew stops and re-quotes before touching anything — never that they keep working and hand you a bigger bill.
