Garage cleanout vs.
junk removal.

They sound like the same service and they are not. One carries things away. The other decides what goes — and that decision is the hard part.

Get a fixed quote Call (407) 735-6450

Junk removal starts after the hard part is done.

If everything is already in a pile by the driveway, you want a hauler. If it is still on the shelves, you want a cleanout.

A junk removal crew arrives, loads what you point at, and drives away. It is fast, it is useful, and it is priced by how much space your pile takes in the truck. What it does not include is the part most people are actually stuck on: opening every box and deciding.

A garage cleanout is the whole job. The crew moves everything out, sorts it with you into keep, donate, and let go, hauls away what is leaving, and sweeps the room before handing it back. You point; they do the lifting and the logistics.

Choosing

Which one is right for you?

Choose junk removal if you have already done the sorting, you know exactly what is leaving, and it is stacked and ready. You are buying muscle and a truck, and you should not pay cleanout prices for that.

Choose a garage cleanout if opening the door is the problem. If the decisions are unmade, the boxes are unopened, or the garage holds decades rather than months, the sorting is the service — and hauling is just the last step of it.

One honest caveat: a cleanout costs more than a haul-away, because it includes hours of labor a truck does not. If your garage is genuinely already sorted, a hauler is the better value and we will tell you so.
